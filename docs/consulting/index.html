Discern Analytics 2.0 consulting

DA2 reporting for behavioral health and post-acute care on Oracle Health’s Continuum and CommunityWorks domains, and DA2 data moved into Google, Azure or your own data warehouse.

I spent four years at Oracle Health, formerly Cerner, building post-acute and value-based care analytics: more than two hundred and fifty revenue cycle reports, and the DA2 training that client teams learned from. I now take on independent Discern Analytics 2.0 work, above all for behavioral health and post-acute organisations on the Continuum and CommunityWorks domains, and for teams that want their DA2 data in a warehouse they control.

Where I specialise

Behavioral health

Reporting for behavioral health and substance use programmes, where lengths of stay run long, levels of care change mid-stay, and some records carry stricter disclosure rules than HIPAA alone. At Oracle Health, behavioral health was part of my client base.

The Continuum domain

Oracle Health’s multi-tenant domain for long-term care, behavioral health and rehabilitation. I have built post-acute revenue cycle, census, scheduling and therapy reporting against it, from inside Oracle and as an independent consultant.

CommunityWorks

The shared, Oracle-hosted domain for community and rural hospitals. On a domain you share and do not administer, DA2 is usually the most direct way to get your own data out, so the reports have to be built to last.

DA2 into Google, Azure and your warehouse

Scheduled DA2 extracts loaded into Google Sheets or BigQuery, Azure SQL or Fabric, Snowflake or another warehouse, and reported from there in Power BI, Tableau or Looker Studio. The data lands somewhere you own and can host your reporting from.

What I do in DA2

Report builds

Queries against Millennium, data cubes, prompts, grouping, crosstabs and calculated columns, built around the question your finance, scheduling or clinical team is actually asking.

Scheduled extracts

DA2 reports that run on a schedule and write their output without anyone pressing Run, picked up by a small pipeline so the numbers are there every morning.

ETL and dashboards

Extracts reshaped, checked and loaded into the warehouse on a schedule, with dashboards on top that show the date of the data they hold and warn when a feed stops arriving.

Report audits

When a total looks wrong it usually is: rows duplicated by a join, a date window on the wrong column, timestamps in a different time zone from the building. I trace the number to its source and make it reconcile.

Training

Hands-on DA2 sessions for the people who write reports and for the managers who run them, on your own domains and data where you allow it.

Questions

What is Discern Analytics 2.0?

DA2 is Oracle Health’s reporting tool for Cerner Millennium: a query and report designer that runs as a desktop application and on the web. It replaced Explorer Menu from Millennium 2018.01, and reports built in it can also be run from the Discern Reporting Portal.

Is DA2 the same as Discern Explorer (CCL)?

No. Discern Explorer is Cerner Command Language, the scripting language programmers write against the Millennium database. DA2 is the designer most report writers and analysts use. Plenty of requests that land in a CCL queue can be built in DA2; when one genuinely needs CCL, I say so while scoping it rather than after.

Can DA2 reports run on their own?

Yes. A DA2 report can be scheduled to run in the background and write its output to a file, which is what makes an automated dashboard possible. The work is mostly in what happens next: picking the right file, knowing which day it describes, and noticing when one does not arrive.
